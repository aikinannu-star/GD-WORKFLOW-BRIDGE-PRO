# Payment Providers & Gateways - Complete Reference

## 🌍 Global Payment Landscape

### Tier 1: Universal Providers (All Regions)

#### **Stripe** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Regions:** 130+ countries
- **Languages:** Multiple
- **Currencies:** 135+
- **Transaction Fee:** 2.9% + $0.30
- **Payout:** 2-4 days
- **Features:** Subscriptions, invoices, tax, connect, radar (fraud)
- **Best for:** Modern SaaS, global reach
- **Website:** https://stripe.com
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

#### **PayPal** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Regions:** 190+ countries
- **Languages:** Multiple
- **Currencies:** 100+
- **Transaction Fee:** 3.49% + $0.49
- **Payout:** 1-2 days
- **Features:** Subscriptions, invoices, disputes, seller protection
- **Best for:** Established brand, global fallback
- **Website:** https://paypal.com
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

## 🌍 Regional Providers

### Africa 🇳🇬 🇬🇭 🇿🇦 🇰🇪

#### **Paystack** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ [PRIMARY]
- **Regions:** Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda
- **Currencies:** NGN, GHS, ZAR, UGX, KES, USD
- **Transaction Fee:** 1.5% + ₦25 (NGN), varies by country
- **Payout:** 1-2 hours (instant available)
- **Payment Methods:** 
  - Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve, AmEx)
  - Bank transfers
  - USSD
  - Wallets
  - Mobile money
- **Features:** Subscriptions, invoices, transfers, disputes
- **Best for:** Nigerian market, West African expansion
- **Website:** https://paystack.com
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

#### **Flutterwave**
- **Regions:** 33+ African countries
- **Currencies:** 20+
- **Transaction Fee:** 1.4% + variable
- **Payout:** 1-2 days
- **Payment Methods:** Cards, bank transfers, mobile money, wallets
- **Features:** Collections, subscriptions, bulk payouts
- **Best for:** Multi-country African operations
- **Website:** https://flutterwave.com
- **Status:** 🔄 READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION

#### **Momo (MTN Mobile Money)**
- **Regions:** Ghana, Cameroon, Ivory Coast, Rwanda, Uganda
- **Currencies:** Local currencies
- **Transaction Fee:** ~5-8%
- **Features:** Mobile money collections
- **Best for:** Unbanked/underbanked populations
- **Website:** https://momoapi.mtn.com
- **Status:** 🔄 READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION

### India & South Asia 🇮🇳 🇧🇩 🇵🇰 🇱🇰

#### **Razorpay** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ [PRIMARY]

## 💡 Specialized Payment Solutions

### Bank Transfers / ACH

#### **Wise (TransferWise)**
- **Regions:** 180+ countries
- **Currencies:** 50+
- **Fee:** 0.41% - 0.84% + base fee
- **Best for:** International payouts, B2B payments

#### **GoCardless**
- **Regions:** Europe, North America, Australia
- **Features:** Direct debits, bank transfers, recurring
- **Transaction Fee:** 0.2% + £0.20 (GBP)
- **Best for:** Subscription billing, recurring payments

#### **Stripe ACH**
- **Regions:** USA
- **Fee:** 0.8% + $0.30
- **Best for:** US domestic transfers, B2B

---

### Digital Wallets

#### **Apple Pay / Google Pay**
- Available through all major gateways
- Reduces friction on mobile
- ~30% improvement in conversion

---

### Crypto Payments

#### **Coinbase Commerce**
- Accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, USDC
- Instant settlement to USD
- 1% fee
- Best for: Crypto-native products

#### **BitPay**
- Accept Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash
- 1% fee
- Settlements in USD, EUR, GBP
